About the Academy

The Decision Intelligence Design Academy (DIDA) is Africa's leading capacity-development ecosystem for decision-intelligent governance, leadership and innovation. We weave leadership and talent development with rigorous training in design, data, AI and emerging technologies—cultivating shared vocabularies, interoperable frameworks, value-driven solutions, and cross-program networks to accelerate responsible, high-impact AI literacy and adoption across public, private, and civic sectors.

What is Decision Intelligence?

Decision intelligence blends data, design and behavioural insights to turn information into transformative action through emerging technologies, and thinks of decision as a Design Intelligence framework that empowers networks with stakeholder engagement and enable communities to thrive.

Decision intelligence sits at the intersection of strategy and impact, empowering teams to thrive in volatile, uncertain, complex and ambiguous contexts. By embedding a culture of innovation, collaboration and ethical leadership, DI-DI drives lasting positive change.

Core Offerings: Decision Intelligence Programs

Positioning Africa's integrative ecosystem for decision-intelligent capacity.

Executive

A modular set of fellowships, masterclasses, micro-courses, and strategic lens designed for executives and senior decision-makers.

Objective: Equip leadership with frameworks to scale innovation from pilots to enterprise.
Need: Address gaps in systems thinking and clear roadmaps for responsible adoption.
Approach: Case studies, diagnostics, innovation labs, roadmap sprints and executive coaching.
Outcomes: Agile governance, organisational capability and networks that reduce digital and human risk.

Emerging Talent

Bootcamps, bootcamps, courses and hackathons for early- and mid-career professionals, innovators, designers and researchers.

Objective: Cultivate cross-disciplinary talent for end-to-end and data-driven innovation.
Need: Bridge deep expertise across innovation gaps with a structured journey to deployment.
Approach: Opportunity mapping, rapid prototyping, hands-on training and accelerated apprenticeships.
Outcomes: Portfolio development, case strategy, visualization and real-world application readiness.

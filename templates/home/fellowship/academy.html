What is Decision Intelligence?

Decision intelligence blends data, design and behavioral insights to turn information into transformative action through emerging technologies. It is a unifying framework that enables cross-functional teams to tackle complex challenges responsibly, scale innovations, and ensure sustainable public, private and civic development.

About the Academy

The Decision Intelligence Design Academy (DIDAcademy) is Africa's leading capacity-development ecosystem for decision-intelligent governance, leadership and innovation. We weave leadership and talent development with rigorous training in design, data, AI and emerging technologies—cultivating shared vocabularies, interoperable frameworks, value-driven solutions, and cross-program networks to accelerate responsible, high-impact AI literacy and adoption across public, private, and civic sectors.

Decision Intelligence Programs

Executive

  • Audience:
    Executives and senior decision-makers
  • Objective:
    Equip leadership with frameworks to scale innovation from pilots to enterprise
  • Approach:
    Case studies, diagnostics, innovation labs, roadmap sprints and executive coaching
  • Outcomes:
    Agile governance, organizational capability and networks that reduce digital and human risk

Emerging Talent

  • Audience:
    Early- and mid-career professionals, innovators, designers and researchers
  • Objective:
    Cultivate cross-disciplinary talent for end-to-end and data-driven innovation
  • Approach:
    Opportunity mapping, rapid prototyping, hands-on training and accelerated apprenticeships
  • Outcomes:
    Portfolio development, case strategy, visualization and real-world application readiness

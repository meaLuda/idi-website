Project Summary

About the prize

The Mombasa Plastics Prize was a youth-led innovation challenge designed to tackle plastic waste mismanagement in informal settlements across Mombasa County, Kenya. Delivered by Challenge Works and implemented by IDI and other partners, the challenge supported young people aged 18-25 to create innovative, viable business solutions.

The program combined field research, design-led capacity building, and enterprise incubation support to enable participants to convert plastic waste into sustainable products, creating environmental and social impact.

Key Insight

"Youth are not the source of the problem, they are the opportunity. They want to lead ownership of the solution in their communities."

The Problem

Plastic waste mismanagement in informal settlements threatens public health and economic opportunities.

Limited waste systems & recycling infrastructure

Inadequate collection points and recycling facilities in high-density informal settlements force illegal dumping and burning.

Low awareness & inconsistent behaviors

Lack of community education on waste segregation, combined with deep-rooted disposal habits, perpetuates trash mismanagement.

High youth unemployment & limited support

Bright young leaders lack structured funding, mentorship, and business incubation channels to scale green innovations.

Our Approach

A human-centered innovation process combining research, design thinking, entrepreneurship and incubation support.

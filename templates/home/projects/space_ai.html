Project Summary

Unlocking Opportunity Through Dairy

Kenya's dairy industry is one of the largest in Africa, contributing 3.8% to the national GDP and providing livelihoods for millions of smallholder farmers. Every day, more than 5 billion liters of milk move through a complex network of farmers, cooperatives, processors, and retailers.

Yet behind the scale and success, many farmers struggle with unpredictable milk supply, low market prices, and limited financial services. SpaceAI is changing that story.

Farmer Feedback

"Now, I know exactly how much milk I deliver, and I get paid fairly. It has changed everything."

The Problem

Many farmers struggle with unpredictable milk supply, low market prices, and limited financial services.

Unpredictable Milk Supply

Farmers lack the tools to accurately track and manage their dairy production, leading to inconsistencies.

Low Market Prices & Transparency

Without clear data, farmers are often victims of middlemen taking hidden cuts and unfair pricing.

Limited Financial Services

Smallholder farmers struggle to access credit and banking services without verifiable production records.

A New Approach: Digital Trust

SpaceAI is pioneering a simple but powerful digital trust platform that helps dairy farmers unlock the true value of their work.
